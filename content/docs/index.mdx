SOMA is not “Python with agents.” It is an attempt to define an abstract machine in which agents, intentions, epistemic values, authority, resources, and effects are native concepts.

## One sentence

> A SOMA program describes an intention, an authorized space of operations, resource limits, epistemic laws, and the conditions required before effects may change the world.

The runtime searches for a valid trajectory inside those boundaries.

## Two questions, two layers

```text
Language semantics
    “May this operation happen?”
    types · lineage · capabilities · requirements · gates

SOMA-VM
    “Which eligible operation should happen now?”
    frames · scheduling · budgets · delegation · convergence
```

## Current normative surface

- Kernel v0 ontology
- Bound attestations and property-specific epistemic flow
- IntentFrame lifecycle and structured `delegate` / `await`
- Budget ownership, settlement, and cancellation
- Sequential ConvergeFrame v0
- SOMA-IR structural contract and lineage/path facts
- SOMA-IR Semantic Ops Phase A: pure core, `read`, and `infer`
- SOMA-IR Semantic Ops Phase B: `verify`, `act`, authority attribution, and atomic gates
- SOMA-IR Semantic Ops Phase C: `delegate`, `await`, and controlled claim → belief internalization
