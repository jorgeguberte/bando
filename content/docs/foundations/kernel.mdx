<Callout type="info" title="FROZEN">
Kernel v0 is the minimal ontology. Runtime and IR types such as `ChildHandle` and `ConvergenceOutcome` do not add new kernel entities.
</Callout>

## Ontology

| Element | Role |
|---|---|
| `agent` | Identity and authority boundary: policies, capabilities, memory, and budget |
| `intent` | Goal-directed unit of computation |
| `operation` | Unit of execution and scheduling |
| `claim<T>` | Communicable epistemic assertion with provenance |
| `evidence` | Observation or artifact that may support or refute a property |
| `attestation<P>` | Authenticated certificate about a predicate and bound subject |
| `belief<T>` | Agent-local internalization of a claim under policy |
| `contract` | Typed input/output boundary and obligations |
| capability | Non-forgeable authority over an effect domain |
| budget | Transactional ledger of delegated resources |

## Short form

> The agent is the unit of authority. The intent is the unit of computation. The operation is the unit of scheduling. The claim is the unit of communicable knowledge.

## Computational classes

```text
fn       deterministic, referentially transparent computation
read[D]  observation of external domain D
infer    epistemic, non-deterministic inference
act[D]   mutation of external domain D
```

The classes do not imply equal risk. `act[sandbox]` and `act[production]` remain different authority domains.
