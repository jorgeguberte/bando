## Editorial rules

1. Dialogue is provenance, not specification.
2. Vendor/model agreement is not formal validation.
3. Counterexamples override rhetorical consensus.
4. Candidate material stays out of normative pages.
5. Every public snapshot identifies its source checkpoint.
6. Resolved material may reopen under implementation pressure.

## Machine-readable access

Fumadocs exposes `llms.txt`, full-text LLM output, per-page Markdown, and local search so models can consume the same curated snapshot as human readers.
